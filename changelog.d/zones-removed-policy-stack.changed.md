The zone is removed from the policy stack: the policy-file compiler no longer produces zone signing keys, tags or six-token permissions, `mashctl add` and `mashctl reissue` sign users with the account key and need no `policy apply` first, and `mashctl policy apply` refuses, with exit 1 and nothing changed, until the label policy model ships (after the guards that refuse an account with an applied zone policy, a zoned directory record or a failing policy test). A policy file that still names a zone (`subject_label`, a `zone` field or a `zone` label) is refused ([ADR 0091](docs/decisions/0091-there-are-no-zones-mail-keeps-the-five-token-subject-and-the-address-is-the-fence.md)). `mashctl policy show`, `policy explain` and `policy diff` are removed with the zone model and are unknown verbs. `mashctl policy repair` reports the `policy apply` refusal at its first step and stops. The JSON output of `mashctl policy drift --json` no longer has a `keys` object (the scoped-key state is gone with the zone keys), so a reader of that JSON should stop expecting it.
