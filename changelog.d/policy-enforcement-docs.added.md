A new document, [docs/policy-enforcement.md](docs/policy-enforcement.md), lets an outside reader check how mash enforces who may mail, prompt, probe and watch whom. It shows how a list set becomes one permission template per owner group plus tags per sender, with output printed by the real compile, what the NATS server checks at connect and on each send, and what a change does to connected senders (the server closes the connections under the changed key and the reconnect is bound by the new template). It also sets out the limits mash cannot remove: the open reply and plumbing subjects, the exception limit by measured size, unsigned labels, key custody, and the test account only wrangler registry. Every claim names the code, test or decision record behind it, and the parts that wait on open pull requests are marked.
