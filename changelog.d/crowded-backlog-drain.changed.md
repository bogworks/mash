A session whose hook is draining its held mail is no longer woken on every settle: a crowded backlog (500 messages or half the byte limit) now wakes at once only when no hook has drained within the idle wake, and the idle spell runs from the last drain. After a drain stops, a crowded backlog waits one idle wake before it wakes the session ([ADR 0069](../docs/decisions/0069-a-crowded-backlog-does-not-wake-a-session-that-a-hook-is-draining.md)).
