A session whose hook is draining its held mail is no longer woken on every settle: a crowded backlog (500 messages or half the byte limit) wakes at once unless a hook drained within the idle wake and the drain is making headroom, meaning the backlog has not grown since it, the stream is not full, and the held size is under half the inbox byte limit. The idle spell runs from the last drain, so a shrinking crowded backlog waits one idle wake after a drain stops ([ADR 0069](../docs/decisions/0069-a-crowded-backlog-does-not-wake-a-session-that-a-hook-is-draining.md)).
