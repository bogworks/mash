**`mash status` says an endpoint is online only once its session has registered it, as `mash who` does.** Before, `mash serve` showed `ready, online` as soon as it held the endpoint, before the agent host's handshake finished and the endpoint answered `mash who`. Until then it says `ready, offline`. ([#1794](https://github.com/bogworks/mash/issues/1794))
