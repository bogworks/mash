`mash serve` now keeps a local, append-only ledger (`presented.log` in the endpoint's spool directory) of what it handed to the host, written before it acks, and `mash status` and the `status` tool report it as `delivery: N presented, M refused, K duplicate, J channel-acked (not proven) since <time>; loss: ...`. Presented means handed to the host's input, not that the model read it; in a session the line also says how many evicted messages the ledger cannot account for. A failed ledger write never holds an ack
([ADR 0109](docs/decisions/0109-serve-keeps-a-local-ledger-of-what-it-presented-written-before-the-ack-and-reported-in-status.md)).
