`mashctl policy show`, `mashctl policy explain SENDER RECEIVER` and `mashctl policy diff FILE` read the list set in force and its status from the policy bucket over the admin connection and change nothing (no lease, no write). `show` prints the list set and how far it is applied, `explain` says whether one address may mail another, and `diff` prints what applying a list-set document would do, prints `accepted: nothing would change` when the document's content is the one in force, and exits 1 when the plan is refused. Each takes `--json`, `--bucket`, `--range` and `--account` (`diff` also `--submitter`), and the bucket comes from the `MASH_POLICY_BUCKET` setting. `mashctl policy` with no verb lists them with `adopt`.
