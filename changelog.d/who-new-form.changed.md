`mash who` now prints a headed table of `ADDRESS` and `STATE`, sorted by address, and `--wide` adds `ROLE`, `HOST` and `DESCRIPTION` (`-` where nothing is recorded). `mash who acme` lists everything under the owner `acme`, `mash who acme.demo` everything under that group, and `mash who acme.demo.bob` that one endpoint, never `acme.demo.bobby`; `acme.*` and `acme.demo.*` mean the same as `acme` and `acme.demo`, and there is no other pattern. The dotted form is the only one: `acme_demo` and `acme/demo` stop with exit 2 and `error: acme_demo is not an address; write it as acme.demo`. `--online` and `--offline` keep their meaning and cannot be given together. With nothing to show it says so: `No endpoints under acme.lab`. `--json` is now the object `{"endpoints": [...]}`, never a bare array, each endpoint with `address`, `online`, `role`, `host` and `description` (`null` where nothing is recorded). The old filters (`--match`, `--name`, `--owner`, `--role`, `--cap`, `--label`) and output modes (`--long`, `--names`, `--format`, the tab-separated lines, the last-seen column) are gone. The MCP `who` tool takes `prefix`, `online`, `offline` and `wide`, and returns the same object as its structured result; its text is the same table. `mash --help` now lists the commands by group: Everyday, Setup, Integration and Raw SAP.
