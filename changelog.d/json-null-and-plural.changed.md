Both tools' `--json` outputs show an unset field as `null` (never `""`, never left out) and name a list in the plural: `mashctl endpoint show` always has `role`, `host`, `description` and `note` and gives `capabilities` (was `capability`), `mashctl init` gives `servers` (was `server`) and `signing_key_pub` as `null` when no key is set, and `mashctl doctor` and `mash version` give `"detail": null` and `"plugin": null` in place of `""`; `mash who` keeps `capability`.
