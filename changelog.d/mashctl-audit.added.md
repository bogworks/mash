`mashctl audit` lists mash's side of the population from the server and the creds directories, read only: every directory record and inbox stream (form, running or stopped, message count, first and last sequence and time, subjects), the orphans (inbox streams, lease keys and creds files, staged ones included, with no directory record), and for every identity with an alias the drain state of its legacy inbox. `--compare EARLIER.json` reports what moved since an earlier `--json` report. `--json` is stable and versioned; `--strict` exits 1 on a finding and 3 means the audit could not complete. It shows state, not use: see [docs/audit.md](docs/audit.md).
