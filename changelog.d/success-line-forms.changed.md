**`mash` and `mashctl` now word a success, an unknown command and a missing thing alike.** A success line has a capital first letter and no full stop (`Mail sent (number 42)`, `Joined as acme.demo.bob`), `mash` says `error: unknown command "x"` with no hint as `mashctl` does, and a missing endpoint or message reads `error: no endpoint <address>` or `error: no message N` in both ([#1046](https://github.com/bogworks/mash/issues/1046)).
