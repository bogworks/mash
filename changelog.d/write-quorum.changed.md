`add`, `reissue` and `retire` no longer need every declared server to answer. A new key, `write_quorum` (`config.json`, `MASH_WRITE_QUORUM`), is `auto` (default: a majority of the declared servers when 3 or more are declared, every server otherwise) or a whole number up to the declared count; a number below a majority is refused when 3 or more are declared. With three servers declared and one down these verbs now work with `MASH_SERVERS` still 3: the newest copy of the account JWT among the answering servers is the base, and a revocation stands when the quorum acknowledged it. Below the quorum they refuse and change nothing (`N of M servers answered and the write quorum is Q: a server may be down: bring it back, or set write_quorum`). `retire` exits 0 with the quorum met and prints a `pending_servers:` line for the servers that have not acknowledged: they take the revocation from the account resolver when they rejoin, and `audit` reports `revocation_pending` until every server holds it. `add` and `reissue` no longer check the account JWT for a zone policy of an older release (zones are removed); `add` needs no system credentials, and `unzone` keeps its check. [ADR 0142](docs/decisions/0142-a-revocation-write-needs-a-quorum-of-the-declared-servers-and-a-server-that-missed-it-takes-it-from-the-resolver.md) records the decision and amends ADR 0067.
