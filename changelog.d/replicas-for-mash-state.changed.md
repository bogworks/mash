Mash-owned streams and buckets (the directory, leases, retired names, issue record, adoption marker and each endpoint's inbox) are now made with a replica count set by one key, `replicas` (`config.json`, `MASH_REPLICAS`, or `--replicas` on `init` and `adopt`): `auto` (the default) is 3 when `servers` (`MASH_SERVERS`) is 3 or more and 1 otherwise; `1` and `3` are the other values and 3 needs three declared servers. `adopt` writes `servers` and `replicas` into `config.json`. An existing object keeps its count, and `store.RaiseReplicas` raises a deployment in place, live, changing only the replica count. A larger replica count than wanted is accepted by the inbox, retired-names, issue-record and adoption checks, a smaller one is refused. `audit` and `issued check` report one finding when the setting wants 3 and an object has fewer. `limits --show` prints the effective replica count. `docs/operations.md` has the replicas section; [ADR 0141](docs/decisions/0141-mash-owned-state-is-replicated-to-the-size-of-the-cluster-set-by-one-key-and-raised-in-place.md) records the decision and amends ADR 0088.
